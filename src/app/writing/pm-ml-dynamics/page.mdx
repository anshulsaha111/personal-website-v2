# The PM's Guide to ML Team Dynamics

Product management for ML teams is different. The sooner you accept this, the more effective you'll be.

## Why It's Different

Traditional product management assumes relatively predictable development cycles. You scope, you build, you ship.

ML breaks this model.

## The Uncertainty Stack

When working with ML teams, you're dealing with stacked uncertainties:

1. **Will the data exist?** Sometimes the data you need hasn't been collected yet.
2. **Will the model work?** Research doesn't always translate to production.
3. **Will users adopt it?** Even good AI gets rejected if it's not positioned correctly.

## Working with ML Engineers

The best ML engineers I've worked with share a trait: they want to solve real problems, not just build cool tech.

Your job is to keep them connected to the problem.

**Bring them to customer conversations.** Let them hear the pain directly.

**Share the constraints upfront.** Latency, cost, explainability—everything affects the solution.

**Celebrate production wins, not just model improvements.** The goal is shipped product, not benchmark scores.

## Managing Up

Executives often don't understand ML timelines. Your job is translation:

- Frame experiments as learning, not failure
- Set expectations about iteration cycles
- Build in contingency for the unknown unknowns
