# The Evaluation Problem No One Talks About

How do you know if your AI system is working?

It sounds like a simple question. It's not.

## The Metrics Trap

Everyone starts with the obvious metrics. Accuracy. Precision. Recall. F1 scores that look great in your slides.

Then you ship, and users hate it anyway.

## What Users Actually Experience

The gap between model metrics and user experience is where most AI products die. A 95% accurate model still fails 1 in 20 times. If your users interact with it 100 times a day, they're getting annoyed five times before lunch.

## A Better Framework

I've started thinking about evaluation in three layers:

**Technical metrics** — The stuff data scientists care about. Necessary but not sufficient.

**Behavioral metrics** — What users actually do. Click-through, completion rates, time-on-task.

**Experiential metrics** — How users feel. Trust, satisfaction, willingness to use again.

Most teams optimize the first layer and wonder why adoption stalls.
